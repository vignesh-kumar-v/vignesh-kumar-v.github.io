01 Profile

Most people stop at "the model works."
I'm interested in what happens after.

I'm Vigneshkumar Venugopal — an MS Data Science candidate at Arizona State University and a former research engineer at ERNET India, where I shipped medical imaging pipelines that radiologists actually used.

My work lives in the unglamorous middle: the fused kernel that makes training 1.7× faster, the quantization scheme that drops a 3B model under 6 GB of VRAM, the memory architecture that stops an agent from contradicting itself three sessions later. I write CUDA when PyTorch is too slow, port models to MLX when CUDA isn't there, and wrap the result in FastAPI, Docker and CI so it survives contact with reality.

Alongside that, I've co-authored two peer-reviewed papers in Elsevier journals on deep learning for renewable-energy systems — a Transformer-LSTM ensemble that beat every RNN baseline we tested, and a multi-objective NSGA-II optimizer for green hydrogen production.

LangGraphQdrantSQLiteOllama

Engram

Biologically-inspired hierarchical memory for LLM coding agents.

Coding agents forget across sessions, and worse, they don't notice when new information contradicts what they already "know." Engram fixes both with an episodic buffer → LLM-driven consolidation (promote / compress / discard) → long-term semantic store architecture, plus a background agent that actively hunts for contradictions. I also wrote LHMBench, an original 6-scenario benchmark, to prove it — it's the only system tested that detected contradictions at all.

  • 100%LHMBench score
  • 1.0 / 0.0vs. RAG on contradictions
  • 336passing tests
  • 8simulated lifecycles

04 Publications

Peer-reviewed work in
Elsevier journals.

Deep learning and evolutionary optimization applied to renewable energy systems.

Renewable Energy · 2025

10.1016/j.renene.2025.122369

A novel ensemble machine learning approach for optimizing sustainability and green hydrogen production in hybrid renewable-based organic Rankine cycle-operated PEM electrolyser systems

  • Designed an ensembled Transformer-LSTM architecture, fusing multi-head attention with LSTM layers to capture both long- and short-range temporal dependencies and sidestep the vanishing-gradient and hyperparameter sensitivity of plain RNNs.
  • Applied integrated gradients for interpretability, confirming the network's attributions aligned with real-world physical constraints — with 5-fold cross-validation for generalizability.
  • Achieved a 0.74% MAPE, outperforming LSTM (0.89%), RNN (1.69%) and FFNN (2.1%) baselines across multi-variable time-series forecasting.
Read the paper
Energy Conversion & Management · 2023

10.1016/j.enconman.2023.117999

Analysis and multi-objective evolutionary optimization of a solar-biogas hybrid cascade Kalina organic Rankine cycle for sustainable cooling and green hydrogen production

  • Built simulation models in Python with CoolProp, automating dynamic thermodynamic property acquisition and continuous 4E (energetic, exergetic, economic, environmental) evaluation under variable boundary conditions.
  • Architected an NSGA-II multi-objective optimization framework across 400 generations, maximizing energy utilization and exergy efficiency while minimizing total system cost — producing a full Pareto frontier.
  • Implemented a two-stage MCDM pipeline — CRITIC for objective weighting, then TOPSIS and MABAC for distance-based ranking — to select the optimal configuration from high-dimensional Pareto data.
